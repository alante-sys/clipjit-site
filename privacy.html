01What ClipJit is

ClipJit is an AI-powered video creation and editing platform for creators. ClipJit is operated by Alante Sturgues, doing business as ClipJit (“ClipJit,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

With ClipJit you can upload or record videos, have them transcribed, find your strongest moments, add captions, effects, and edits, plan your content, and — only when you choose — publish to social accounts you connect.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ClipJit collects when you use the ClipJit website and app at clipjit.com, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to everyone who uses ClipJit, including when you connect Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts.

02Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run ClipJit:

Account and profile
Your email address, your workspace name, sign-in and session records, and preferences such as your theme and default settings.
Workspace and brand settings
Team membership, brand kit items (colors, fonts, and logos you upload), style packs, and content plans you create.
Content you upload or create
Videos and audio you upload or record, transcripts and captions generated from them, edits, effects, renders and exported files, thumbnails, hooks, titles, and descriptions.
Publishing information
Captions, hashtags, schedules, the accounts you choose, approval decisions, and the post IDs and links a platform returns after something is published.
Performance and analytics
Performance metrics you enter, and metrics a connected platform returns where that platform provides them.
Connected-account metadata
For Facebook and Instagram: your Facebook Page ID and name, your Instagram professional account ID and username, the permissions you granted, and when the access token expires. We store similar identifiers for other platforms you connect.
Device and log data
IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, request times, and error logs, used to operate and secure ClipJit.
Cookies
An essential session cookie that keeps you signed in, and your theme preference stored in your browser. ClipJit does not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics trackers, or tracking pixels.

ClipJit does not ask for or store payment card numbers. If paid plans are offered, payments are handled by a payment processor.

03How we use information

  • Provide ClipJit's features: transcription, finding moments, captions, edits, effects, renders, and exports.
  • Connect the social accounts you authorize and publish the content you approve.
  • Show your content calendar, publishing history, and performance insights.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your content or data from connected platforms for advertising or to build profiles about you.

04How ClipJit uses Meta, Facebook, and Instagram APIs

ClipJit uses Meta's official Facebook Graph API and Instagram Graph API through Facebook Login. Connecting is optional and happens only when you choose Connect with Facebook in ClipJit and approve access on Meta's authorization screen.

When you connect, you may authorize ClipJit to access Facebook Pages you manage and the Instagram professional accounts (Business or Creator accounts) linked to those Pages. You choose which Pages and accounts to share on Meta's authorization screen, and you can change or remove that access later.

ClipJit may request the following permissions:

instagram_basic · Instagram
Read your Instagram professional account's basic profile (account ID and username) so ClipJit can show and verify which account you connected.
instagram_content_publish · Instagram
Publish Reels and posts you approve to your Instagram professional account, and check whether each post finished publishing.
pages_show_list · Instagram & Facebook Page
List the Facebook Pages you manage so you can choose which Page to connect (Instagram professional accounts are linked to a Page).
pages_read_engagement · Instagram & Facebook Page
Read basic information about the Page you choose, including its linked Instagram professional account, to set up and verify the connection.
pages_manage_posts · Facebook Page
Publish Reels and posts you approve to your Facebook Page.
business_management · Instagram & Facebook Page
Access the Pages and Instagram professional accounts you manage through a Meta business portfolio, so they appear when you connect.

ClipJit uses this access only to:

  • Identify the Facebook Page and Instagram professional account you connected, and confirm the connection works.
  • Publish Reels and posts to that Page or Instagram account — only content you authorize.
  • Check whether a post ClipJit published for you succeeded, so ClipJit can show its status.

ClipJit does not read your personal Facebook profile, friends list, or messages; it does not post anything you haven't authorized; and it does not currently request Meta insights permissions. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for your authorization first.

Data ClipJit receives from Meta is used only to provide the features you use. It is never sold, never used for advertising or profiling, never shared with data brokers, and is handled in accordance with Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

05ClipJit only publishes when you authorize it

Connecting an account does not publish anything. ClipJit publishes content only when you authorize it.

  • By default, every post requires your approval before it can be published.
  • If you turn on scheduling or automation rules, ClipJit publishes only within the accounts, content types, and schedules you set. You can pause or turn them off at any time.
  • ClipJit never publishes to an account you haven't connected, and never uses a connection for anything other than the posts you create in ClipJit.
  • Connection checks in ClipJit, such as Test Connection and Run Real Validation, verify access without publishing.

06How OAuth access tokens are stored and protected

When you connect Facebook, Instagram, or another platform, that platform gives ClipJit an OAuth access token. ClipJit protects it as follows:

  • Tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM using a dedicated encryption key kept as a server secret, separate from the database.
  • Tokens are decrypted only in server memory when ClipJit calls the platform for you. They are never sent to your browser, shown in the ClipJit interface, or written to logs.
  • The platform app credentials ClipJit uses to talk to Meta are also encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • ClipJit records when each token expires and asks you to reconnect when it does.
  • When you disconnect an account in ClipJit, the stored tokens for that account are deleted immediately.
  • Access to production systems is limited to authorized personnel.

07How we process videos, captions, publishing information, analytics, and account metadata

Uploaded videos and audio
Stored in private cloud storage and accessed through short-lived signed links. ClipJit processes them to create transcripts, find moments, render edits, and detect where faces appear in the frame so captions and graphics don't cover them. Face detection only locates faces; ClipJit does not identify people or create face recognition templates.
Transcripts and captions
Created with a speech-to-text provider and used to build captions, hooks, titles, and edits. You can review and edit them before anything is published.
AI analysis
Transcripts and content details may be sent to an AI model provider to suggest hooks, moments, captions, and effects. ClipJit does not sell your content and does not use it to train AI models.
Publishing information
Captions, hashtags, schedules, approvals, and the post IDs platforms return are stored so you can see what was published, when, and where.
Analytics
Metrics you enter, or that connected platforms return, are stored in your workspace to show performance and improve future suggestions for you. We don't combine them with data from other sources to profile you.
Account metadata
Page IDs and names, Instagram account IDs and usernames, granted permissions, and token expiry are stored to show which accounts are connected and to publish to the right place.

  • Service providers that process data on our behalf under agreements that limit how they can use it (listed below).
  • Platforms you connect, when you ask ClipJit to publish or to verify a connection.

10Security practices

  • Encryption in transit with HTTPS (TLS).
  • AES-256-GCM encryption at rest for access tokens and platform app credentials.
  • Private storage for media files, accessed through short-lived signed links.
  • Workspace isolation: your data is scoped to your workspace, and every request is checked for authorization.
  • Protection against cross-site request forgery and abuse, including same-origin checks and rate limiting.
  • Tokens and secrets are redacted from logs and error messages.
  • Least-privilege access to production systems.

No system is perfectly secure. If a security incident affects your personal information, we will notify you and the appropriate authorities as required by law.

12How to disconnect Facebook and Instagram

In ClipJit
Go to Integrations (or Provider Activation), find Instagram or Facebook, and choose Disconnect. ClipJit deletes the stored access tokens immediately and stops publishing to that account.
On Facebook
Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (on some accounts this is Apps and websites). Select ClipJit and choose Remove.
On Instagram
Open Instagram and go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites. Under Active, select ClipJit and choose Remove.

Removing ClipJit in Facebook or Instagram revokes its access at Meta. Meta may rename these menus over time. To also delete the data ClipJit already stores, follow the Data Deletion Instructions.

14Your rights

Depending on where you live — for example, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or U.S. states such as California — you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, including by disconnecting an account. This doesn't affect processing that already happened.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To exercise a right, email alante@xtramilecoverage.com. We'll verify your request and respond within the time required by applicable law, usually within 30 days. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
